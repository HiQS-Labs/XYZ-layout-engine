# Stop Re-Rolling the Whole Image — design brief

## Overview

Source: GitHub issue HiQS-Labs/XYZ-layout-engine#69, its timed 18-minute outline and verified nutrition demo.

- **Reader:** a technical talk audience that has seen generative infographics but has not used XYZ Layout Engine.
- **Takeaway:** AI can author the design once; structured fixtures and trusted recipes make later edits exact and repeatable.
- **Most space:** the subject of each slide — the re-roll schematic, ownership split, system and workflow diagrams, live commands, real before/after renders, and versioning stories.

Hierarchy decisions:

1. An 86 px title leads every slide; one diagram, comparison or command sequence occupies the central band.
2. Orange marks the actor or field requiring attention; navy carries deterministic engine behavior. Neutral rules organize copy before panels.
3. Slides 4 and 7 are evidence slides: their density goes to verified components, real renders, commands and hashes rather than decoration.

Every slide is a separate JSON fixture. `render-deck.mjs` owns geometry and composes scenes for the shared `renderSatori` and `renderPlaywright` operations. There is no hand-drawn slide image, image-model output, new published recipe or new dependency.

## Colors

`ground` is the warm off-white canvas; `ink` is primary navy type; `muted` is secondary copy; `accent` is the single orange attention color; `accentSoft` marks AI or requested-edit regions; `rule` separates sections; `panel` and `soft` are solid fills for genuinely distinct diagram stages. No gradients, glows, glass, blobs or sparkles.

## Typography

Vendored Barlow Condensed Regular/Bold (`tools/fonts/barlow-condensed/`) is the single family; all copy is short, including commands. The script computes every size as `round(S × pct)`, where `S = min(1920, 1080) = 1080`:

| Role | % of S | px | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Headline | 8.0% | 86 | slide titles and rare closing statements |
| Subhead | 3.0% | 32 | subtitles, column heads, key comparisons |
| Label | 2.4% | 26 | stage titles, eyebrows, commands needing emphasis |
| Body | 1.9% | 21 | captions, command lines, sources |

Four sizes; headline : body = 4.10 : 1. Essential slide meaning is carried by titles, subheads and labels. Body copy is supportive and will be reviewed honestly at 1/3 scale and 400 px width.

## Layout

Canvas is 1920 × 1080 (16:9), with an 88 px outer margin and an 8 px spacing rhythm. A common header and source/footer band frame a central content band. Compositions vary by content: three-stage re-roll schematic, unequal ownership columns, left-to-right system and workflow diagrams, command ledger, two-image comparison, version flow, and a two-column close. Panels are reserved for distinct stages or artifacts, not used as a generic tile grid.

## Elevation & Depth

No elevation effects. Hierarchy comes from type, spacing, solid fills and 2 px rules. The real nutrition renders retain their own internally produced artwork and shadows; this deck adds no shadow treatment to them.

## Shapes

Diagram panels share one 12 px radius and one 2 px outline. Connectors are straight horizontal or vertical lines with filled arrowheads. The re-roll slide is explicitly labelled as a schematic, not a model comparison.

## Components

- **Common frame:** eyebrow, title, optional subtitle, source note and slide number.
- **Diagram stage:** a titled solid panel with concise caption; arrows show actual reading direction.
- **Command ledger:** exact issue #69 commands as text, not screenshots.
- **Real comparison:** committed before/after nutrition PNGs generated by the issue commands, with matching SVG hash and measured wall times.
- **Narrative lists:** ownership, recipe rules, drive modes and takeaways use alignment and rules rather than ornamental cards.

## Artwork provenance

No artwork is generated for this deck. Slides 1–6 and 8–10 use text, HTML elements and inline vector connectors. Slide 7 embeds only real local outputs from `nutrition@4.0.0`, produced with Node v26.0.0 by the verified issue #69 commands. `demo-receipt.json` records their source manifests, digests and timings.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep all claims tied to issue #69, current code and actual renders.
- Do disclose that slide 2 is a schematic and that slide 9's agent, not the engine, translates DESIGN.md.
- Do name the current-code location of geometry checks; the README's `tools/spike/measure-chromium.mjs` reference is absent in this checkout and is not drawn as a real component.
- Don't use “milliseconds”; the measured stage wording is “under a second.”
- Don't relax text-fit, overlap or clipping checks to make a slide pass.
- Don't shrink below the four-role type scale; shorten copy or revise geometry instead.

### AS-12 exception

Slide 7's two 480 px real-render images exceed the default illustration-to-headline-cap ratio. This is a content-driven exception: the audience must compare actual headline and color changes in the engine outputs. They are evidence, not decorative hero artwork; the title, captions, hash and timing remain independently legible. The measured ratio is reported rather than hidden.
