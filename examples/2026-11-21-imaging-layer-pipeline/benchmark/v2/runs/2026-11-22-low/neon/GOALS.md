# Reader and delivery goals

A newcomer should be able to point to each edit, explain what changes on the poster, and trace the direction of one PNG export. Choose a clear visual entry point at thumbnail size. The reader can use pictures, lettering or both. Detail earns its place when it helps the reader complete a task.

Deliver a decodable PNG (short side at least 800 pixels, at most 16 megapixels, at least eight distinct quantized sample colors), and matching self-contained offline HTML with decoded images and no script errors. Limit each export to 64 MiB. The build should regenerate both in a fresh copy. Editing a caption and rebuilding must visibly change both.
