# Ridgeline field notes

Make a portrait field-note poster for hikers learning to read a landscape. The takeaway is: a ridge divides the direction water flows. This is an illustrative explanation, not a surveyed route or safety guide.

Use an 800 by 1040 canvas. Use warm paper, dark charcoal ink and a muted rust accent for the divide. Use bundled Barlow Condensed in regular and bold, an 8px spacing grid, square corners and no shadows.

The title READ THE RIDGE deserves first attention, with one large schematic ridge illustration below it. Give the explanation generous space beneath the illustration; keep the source/disclosure quiet at the bottom. No equal card grid or decorative badges.

Create one separate PNG: a side view of a ridge, simplified dark slopes with the divide marked in rust. Use flat illustrative lighting on a transparent background. Exclude labels, lettering, arrows, people and photographic texture. Keep explanatory text outside the PNG. A synthetic diagram placeholder is acceptable for this pipeline example, but disclose it.

Use this copy:
- RIDGELINE / FIELD NOTES
- READ THE RIDGE
- A ridge divides the direction water flows.
- Follow the shape of the land: water moves down either side of the divide.
- Schematic illustration. Synthetic PNG; no image model used.
