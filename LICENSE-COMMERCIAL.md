## Using the engine under the AGPL

You can use the engine to render infographics, diagrams and slide decks, including for commercial projects. Section 2 covers generated output only when the output itself constitutes a covered work. For example, you can use the engine to render your own artwork for a client. Check the terms for any fonts, images or software you include in the deliverable.

If you modify the engine and your version supports remote interaction, section 13 requires you to prominently offer everyone interacting with it remotely over a network access to the Corresponding Source of your version, at no charge. This is relevant, for example, when users submit render requests to a modified engine through a hosted service. If you distribute copies of the engine, sections 4–6 describe the applicable notice, licensing and source obligations. See the [full license](LICENSE).

## When to ask about commercial terms

Contact Neochrome if you want to distribute the engine as part of a proprietary design tool, operate a modified rendering service under different source-sharing terms, or use the software in an organization whose policies require a commercial agreement.

Email **support@neochro.me** with the subject **Commercial license — XYZ Layout Engine**. Describe your organization, how you intend to deploy or modify the engine, who will use it, and whether you need hosting rights, redistribution rights or both. The signed agreement defines the rights granted and the price.
